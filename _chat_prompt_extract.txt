1. Executive Summary
The project began as the public website and operational dashboard for African Home Adventure (AHA), a Kenya/Tanzania tour operator.
The system has since evolved into a broader vision: Enzi, a tourism and hospitality-focused technology company that will develop a scalable SaaS platform capable of supporting multiple companies, industries, countries, websites, and business processes.
The strategic direction is therefore:
Build Enzi as a tourism/hospitality-focused SaaS platform, initially validated through African Home Adventure, with AI-powered safari itinerary generation as a major commercial product.
The platform will become the central source of operational and business information for companies using it.
The companies' public websites will remain customer-facing systems, while Enzi provides the underlying business infrastructure.

25. Architectural Principles
I recommend we formally adopt these principles.
Principle 1 — AHA is a tenant, not the platform
No AHA-specific assumptions should exist in the core architecture.
Principle 2 — Enzi is the source of truth
Websites consume platform data.
Principle 3 — APIs are first-class
The dashboard and websites should eventually consume the same domain services/API capabilities.
Principle 4 — Tenant isolation is mandatory
No cross-company access.
Principle 5 — Configuration over hardcoding
Companies should be configured rather than hardcoded.
Principle 6 — Modules over duplicated applications
Tourism, hospitality and future industries should be modules.
Principle 7 — AI assists; it does not become the source of truth
AI recommendations must be grounded in platform data.
Principle 8 — Financial data requires strong consistency
Payments, invoices and financial records require transactional integrity.
Principle 9 — Build for multiple countries
Country, currency, tax and location must be configurable.
Principle 10 — Build incrementally
Do not rewrite working software without a business reason.
